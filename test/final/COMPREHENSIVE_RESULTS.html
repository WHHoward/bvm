D. Key Findings

QuestionAnswer
Why I_SL drops from 32μA→17μA in 4-BVM? 80% from multi-BVM leakage (~12μA lost into 3 non-reading BVM R-loops), 20% from load impedance (~3μA: 12×jj320→1×jj320+QB). Isolation test proves single BVM+QB still pulls 29-31μA.
How many SFQ pulses does QB output? Zero discrete pulses. Phase is continuous. Max accumulated: 1.00-1.55Φ₀ (with 4 cells). No 2π resets. The 10ps BVM pulse is too short for the QB junction to complete a full SFQ transition.
JS=18μA vs JS=25μA? JS=25μA gives higher I_SL (+11% at Read4), much better phase reset (0.31 vs 3.31 rad残留), but lower phase accumulation (0.87 vs 1.55Φ₀). JS=25μA is preferred for clean multi-read operation.
Best R_shunt? R=7Ω: highest I_SL, cleanest reset. R=15Ω: highest phase (1.00Φ₀). Trade-off: no configuration achieves discrete 2π SFQ.
Does V_OUT show any output? No. V_OUT < 0.03mV in all tests. The QB output junction (JL2) never triggers.

E. Fundamental Limitation

The BVM read current pulse is ~10ps wide (FWHM) during single-cell read, dropping to ~3-4ps during multi-cell reads. To generate one SFQ pulse (Φ₀ = 2.07 mV·ps), the QB junction needs to sustain V_JS above ~0.2mV for ~10ps. Currently V_JS peaks at only 0.3mV and the pulse is too short.

Bottom line: The QB circuit in its current form does NOT convert BVM analog current levels into discrete SFQ pulse counts. The phase accumulation is continuous and doesn't reach clean 2π multiples. This is the "modification" the MVM paper alludes to but doesn't detail — our results suggest a non-trivial redesign of the QB front-end is needed for BVM interfacing.
